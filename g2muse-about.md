# G2Muse — About

**G2Muse** brings your AI assistant to your Even Realities G2 glasses. Messages appear right in your field of view — and you can reply by voice without touching your phone.

**How it works:** G2Muse connects to a private message relay (your own secret GitHub Gist). Your assistant posts messages to the relay; the app picks them up and shows them on your glasses. No accounts, no bots, no third-party servers — just your relay.

**Features:**
- **Messages on glasses** — text from your assistant, readable at a glance
- **Voice replies** — hold the ring, talk, release. Your message is sent as audio and transcribed automatically
- **Live clock** — always visible top-right, updating every 30 seconds
- **Timestamps** — every message shows sender, date and time
- **Read tracking** — see unread counts; messages mark as read when viewed
- **14 languages** — auto-detected from your phone, or set manually
- **Private by design** — your relay credentials never leave your phone

**Setup (one time):** create a free secret gist, generate a GitHub token, paste both into the app. Done in under five minutes. See `g2muse-opsætning.md`.
